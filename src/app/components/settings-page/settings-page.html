Encryption

Everything you've imported and entered is encrypted on this device with a key protected by your passphrase (AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2 with 600,000 iterations). The app asks for the passphrase when it starts and locks itself when idle. If you forget the passphrase, the data can't be recovered. Keep an export somewhere safe.

AI provider (optional)

Off by default; the app is complete without it. When on, it can suggest categories for transactions you haven't labelled, map an unusual budget spreadsheet, and power the Advisor. Every call shows you exactly what will be sent first. Names, account numbers and exact amounts are removed.

No key to find or copy: sign in (or sign up) at OpenRouter, approve Crux, and you come back here with a key for this session. You pay OpenRouter for what you use, and can set a limit or revoke the key there. Like any key here, it's forgotten when the app reloads or locks: sign in again then.

Your key stays in memory until the app reloads or locks. It's never written to this device or included in exports, and it's sent only to this provider. Use a dedicated key with a low spending limit, which you can revoke at any time in the provider's console.

Investment return history

Simulate an investment with its own price history instead of the built-in assumptions. Download a CSV of monthly or daily prices (for example from Yahoo Finance: Date and Close columns), in Australian dollars, and add it here. It's read on this device; nothing is fetched. Then pick it as a holding's returns on the Freedom screen.
