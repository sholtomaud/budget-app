Privacy

  • Your data stays in this browser. There is no server, no account and no cloud sync.
  • The app makes no network request except to load its own files, unless you configure an external service and confirm each call.
  • No analytics and no third-party scripts.
  • This is not financial advice. The app simulates the consequences of your own assumptions, and shows every assumption it uses.
