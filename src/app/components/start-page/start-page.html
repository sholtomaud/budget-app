How it works

  1. Add your data, or try a sample household.
  2. Check the categories Crux has given your spending.
  3. Set your budget and your goal: the life you want, and when.
  4. Compare plans and see which ones are most likely to work. A guide at the top of each screen walks you through.

Your data stays with you

  • Everything stays in this browser. No account, no server, nothing uploaded.
  • You can encrypt it with a passphrase, export it, or delete it all in Settings.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • If you turn on an AI assistant, you see exactly what would be sent and approve every request.
  • Crux isn't financial advice. It shows the consequences of your own assumptions, and every number says where it came from.
