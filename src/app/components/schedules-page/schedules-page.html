Schedules

Things Crux does on its own at set times: check your budget each morning, run your plan each week, remind you to import. They run while Crux is open in a tab; anything that fell due while it was closed runs once when you open it. A schedule asks the chat, so you see what it did, and it never changes your data: any change is a card for you to apply.

Settings

Off: a schedule that needs the AI is skipped, and the AI is only called when you ask. On: it calls your AI provider without asking each time, with amounts redacted as always, and each call is in the call log, marked as scheduled. Your API key is only kept until the app reloads, so after a reload these are skipped until you enter it again (a local model needs none).
